Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the MtzNet home automation application (the “Application”) handles information.

Overview

The Application is a private, self-hosted home automation instance operated by the MtzNet administrator. It is not a commercial service and has no external users. It integrates with the Google account of its operator.

Information accessed

With the operator’s explicit OAuth consent, the Application accesses:

  • Gmail — read, search, send and organise the operator’s mail
  • Google Calendar — read and manage the operator’s events
  • Google Drive — read and manage the operator’s files
  • Google Contacts — read the operator’s contact details
  • Google Sheets & Docs — read and edit the operator’s documents

Use of information

All data accessed from Google services is used solely to provide automation requested by the operator — for example sending email, checking the calendar, organising files, and generating summaries. Data is processed locally on self-hosted infrastructure and is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning or AI models.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and cached data are stored locally on the operator’s self-hosted infrastructure only. There is no third-party database. Data is retained only as long as needed for the requested automation, and access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

Data sharing

The Application does not transfer user data to any third party. No human other than the operator can read the accessed data.
