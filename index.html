Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the “Tiberio Martinez Personal Tools” application (the “Application”) handles your information.

Overview

The Application is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by Tiberio Martínez Ardila (“I”, “me”). It is not a commercial service and has no other users. It integrates with the Google account of its sole operator.

Information accessed

With the account owner’s explicit OAuth consent, the Application accesses:

  • Gmail — read, search, send and organise the account owner’s mail
  • Google Calendar — read and manage the account owner’s events
  • Google Drive — read and manage the account owner’s files
  • Google Contacts — read contact details of the account owner
  • Google Sheets & Docs — read and edit the account owner’s documents

Use of information

All data accessed from Google services is used solely to provide automation requested by the account owner — for example sending email, checking the calendar, organising files, and generating summaries. Data is processed on the account owner’s own devices and is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning or AI models.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and cached data are stored locally on the account owner’s devices only. There is no server-side database. Data is retained only as long as needed for the requested automation, and the account owner can revoke access at any time via Google Account permissions.

Data sharing

The Application does not transfer user data to any third party. No human other than the account owner can read the accessed data.
